# YouTube patches v1.48.2

## New: Audio equalizer
- In-app EQ under Morphe settings → Video → Audio equalizer.
- Device-supported band sliders, hardware presets, enable/disable and reset to flat.
- Saved frequency gains restored on audio-session changes.
- Only positive YouTube audio sessions are used; the global output mix is never modified.
- Unsupported devices/audio engines show a status rather than forcing effects.

- Recommended stable YouTube: **21.23.492** (unchanged).
